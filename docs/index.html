RiskStriker is being developed around three practical questions.

01 / CONTEXT

What is at stake?

Map the agent's permissions, tools and actions against the organisation's risk tolerance.

02 / CONSEQUENCE

What actually happened?

Measure the records accessed, changes made or messages sent. An attempt alone is not the outcome.

03 / CONTROL

What changed after the fix?

Repeat the scenario after remediation and compare the observed impact.

INITIAL FOCUS

Consequential agents.
Public-sector context.

We're starting with the security and resilience questions facing teams developing action-taking public-sector agents. The work is at the R&D stage.
