We're building RiskStriker.

Cybernetic is developing an operational assurance platform for autonomous AI agents. The product is in development and is not available for production use.

OUR FOCUS

Understand the impact
of agent autonomy.

As agents gain access to tools, data and consequential actions, organisations need evidence about how the complete system behaves under failure.

RiskStriker brings together agent context, cyber and operational testing, consequence measurement and control verification. Our initial focus is action-taking public-sector agents and the evidence needed for context-specific risk decisions.
