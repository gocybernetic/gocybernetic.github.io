THE DECISION

What authority?
Under which controls?

Useful assurance starts with the deployment decision your organisation needs to make. The proposed method connects that decision to explicit risk boundaries and observable consequences.

Practitioners

Reproduce meaningful failures, inspect downstream state and retest the controls after remediation.

Security leadership

Review material red-line breaches, control gaps and the limits of the evidence.

Governance teams

Bring a scoped assessment into the existing risk and deployment-approval process.

THE LIMIT OF THE CLAIM

A test result is evidence. It is not a guarantee.

Results apply to the configuration, environment and scenarios assessed. Untested areas, unresolved failures and inconclusive results belong in the conclusion. The organisation remains responsible for risk acceptance.

RiskStriker is in development. Initial scope excludes production runtime defence, automatic remediation and certification.
