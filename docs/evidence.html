METHODOLOGY / EVIDENCE

A conclusion you can trace back to the test.

RiskStriker’s planned evidence pack brings the system, risk definition, scenarios and measured consequences into one reviewable record.

System & configuration

Agent version, permissions, autonomy, controls, configuration fingerprint and test date.

Risk & coverage

Risk tolerance, red lines, tested and partially tested areas, plus what was not tested or not observable.

Environment

Identify real, test-equivalent, simulated and out-of-scope components. Do not hide simulation.

Observed impact

Attempted and realised actions, trial counts, maximum observed impact, containment and recovery.

Control verification

Link baseline, customer remediation and the same-scenario retest. Report the measured difference.

Remaining uncertainty

Record unresolved failures, inconclusive results, assumptions and limitations alongside the conclusion.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE DOES NOT SAY

Tested does not mean universally safe.

An assessment does not certify an agent or guarantee future behaviour. Coverage, unresolved failures, inconclusive results and simulated components remain part of the conclusion. Risk acceptance stays with the customer.

Initial scope excludes production runtime defence, a kill switch and automatic remediation.
