THE EVIDENCE APPROACH

Show the result. Keep the context.

The planned RiskStriker evidence pack connects each conclusion to the system and scenarios tested.

01 / System

Agent version, tools, permissions, autonomy, control configuration and assessment date.

02 / Risk boundaries

The organisation’s risk tolerance, red lines and required failure behaviour.

03 / Environment

Which components were real, test-equivalent, simulated or outside scope.

04 / Coverage

Tested, partially tested, not tested and not observable areas. Scenario quantity alone does not establish assurance quality.

05 / Consequence

Attempted and realised actions, trial counts, maximum observed impact, containment and recovery.

06 / Control verification

The baseline, customer control change, identical-scenario retest and measured difference.

07 / Residual uncertainty

Unresolved failures, inconclusive results, assumptions and limitations.

08 / Tested conditions

A bounded conclusion for the configuration and scenarios assessed, ready for the organisation’s risk review.

THE LIMIT OF THE CLAIM

A test result is evidence. It is not a guarantee.

Results apply to the configuration, environment and scenarios assessed. Untested areas, unresolved failures and inconclusive results belong in the conclusion. The organisation remains responsible for risk acceptance.

RiskStriker is in development. Initial scope excludes production runtime defence, automatic remediation and certification.
